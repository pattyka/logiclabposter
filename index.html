Common questions
How does the app handle screen time?

It uses Apple’s Screen Time API, which lets the app talk directly to iOS screen-time management. Once your child finishes their tasks, the amount of screen time you set unlocks automatically. The Android version uses Digital Wellbeing and the Accessibility Services API for the same thing.

Why sign up if the app is not out yet?

Signing up helps me show there is real demand for this. I am preparing for a competition where I have to back up with data that parents care about their children’s critical thinking. Every sign-up is a vote for this project getting built.

Is the trial really free?

Yes. The 7-day trial needs no card. When the app is out you get full access to everything for the first week, and after that you decide whether to carry on.

Which devices will it work on?

It arrives on iOS first, iPhone and iPad, with the Android version close behind. The parent side will also be reachable from a web browser.

Is my child’s data handled safely?

Data protection comes first. Screen time is handled on the device itself, and only anonymised learning statistics reach the server. It complies fully with GDPR and with children’s data-protection rules.
