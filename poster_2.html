Experimental and method

LogicLab Kids · EUCYS 2026
Native Platforms 01

Native SwiftUI (MVVM) on iOS (live on Apple TestFlight) and Kotlin Compose on Android. Operates offline-first; all lessons execute with zero network connectivity.

Stage Grammar 02

6 modules and 36 lessons bundled as JSON. 4 interactive task types: Quiz, Spot-It, Sort-It, Build-It. Mistakes are never punished (explanations shown after 2 misses).

Premack Contingency 03

Applies the Premack (1959) differential probability law. Solving a lesson credits the ledger with +20 pts & +5 min. Child spends balance within parent-allowed hours.

Constrained Gemini 04

System prompt restricts Gemini to Socratic dialogue: probes reasoning and gives hints, never direct answers. Strict low token cap prevents lecturing; unlocks only post-task.

The Behavioral Lock

Screen entertainment is the highest-probability behavior; structured reasoning practice is among the lowest. The lock and key are in the OS: a child cannot negotiate around it.

Opaque Token Shielding

The system picker returns opaque tokens, so LogicLab Kids never accesses which apps the parent selected. Shields are applied directly by iOS ManagedSettings, not in-app.

4 Core Task Types

Quiz (sound continuation), Spot-It (faulty step), Sort-It (inference chain), and Build-It (conclusion assembly). The child acts dynamically rather than memorizing definitions.
