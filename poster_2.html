Experimental and method

LogicLab Kids · EUCYS 2026
Native Platforms 01

Native SwiftUI (MVVM) on iOS, live on TestFlight; Kotlin Compose on Android. All 36 lessons ship as bundled JSON, so a lesson runs end to end with the network off; the cloud holds only identity, progress and the tutor.

Stage Grammar 02

6 courses × 6 lessons × 5 stages = 180 stages. Every lesson opens on a story and closes on the AI challenge; between them, three tasks drawn from five types: Quiz, Spot-It, Sort-It, Build-It, Grid. A miss costs nothing: after two, the explanation is shown.

Premack Contingency 03

Premack (1959): the higher-probability behaviour reinforces the lower one. A solved lesson pays +5 min and 20–25 pts into a ledger the child spends inside parent-allowed hours; an Early Bird deadline set by the parent multiplies the payout.

Constrained Gemini 04

The challenge is the app’s only AI surface and always the last stage. The system prompt forbids stating the answer and caps every reply at three sentences; a low token ceiling stops it lecturing. The child must make at least three moves.

Why the lock is in the OS

Making entertainment contingent on reasoning only works if it cannot be negotiated. So the lock and the key are both in iOS, not in the app: a nine-year-old cannot talk his way around it, and the parent stops being the confiscator.

Opaque Token Shielding

The system picker returns opaque tokens, so LogicLab Kids never accesses which apps the parent selected. Shields are applied directly by iOS ManagedSettings, not in-app.

Inside a Lesson Story → Task → Explanation → Challenge
FIG 4. The “Slippery slope” lesson: the concept arrives inside a scene the child knows, becomes a task the child must solve, and a wrong answer ends in an explanation, never a penalty. The AI tutor comes last, after the child has done the work.
5 Core Task Types

Quiz (sound continuation), Spot-It (faulty step), Sort-It (inference chain), Build-It (conclusion assembly) and Grid (entity–attribute matching). In every type the child acts on the argument instead of reading a definition of it.
